The terms of working with us.

What this website is for, what WOTTT does and does not promise, and how quotations, bookings and payments work.

Who we are

This website is run by Wolf of the Travel Trade (“WOTTT”), trading as WOTTT Travel & Tours from branches in Lahore (DTS Licence No. 12267).

Using this website

The information on this website — study destinations, costs, timelines, visa requirements, package details — is general guidance. Rules, fees and requirements change; we review our pages regularly, but always confirm the current position with a consultant before you act on it.

Acceptable use

Use this website for lawful purposes only. Do not submit false information, another person’s details without their permission, or anything intended to disrupt the website or our enquiry system.

Planners and estimates

The Umrah package builder, the budget planner, the country comparison and the intake calendar are planning aids. What they show is indicative. Nothing produced by them is a quotation, an offer or a booking; a WOTTT quotation is always issued to you in writing.

Consultation is guidance

Consultation is guidance only. WOTTT does not guarantee admissions, scholarships or visa outcomes. Universities decide admissions and scholarships; embassies, consulates and Saudi authorities decide visas.

Quotations and bookings

No booking is made until you confirm it. A quotation is valid for the period stated on it; prices for flights, hotels and packages depend on availability at the moment of booking.

  • Payment: the written quotation sets out the accepted payment method, deposit and balance deadline for your booking. Make payments only through the WOTTT payment instructions issued for your case and keep the receipt. Third-party charges are identified separately where applicable.
  • Cancellations and refunds: charges depend on the flights, hotels, visa work and other services already booked. WOTTT states the deposit, balance due date and applicable refund terms in your quotation before payment. Ask us to explain any non-refundable item before confirming.
  • Airlines, hotels, insurers and other providers have their own conditions, which also apply to your booking; we give you them before you pay.

Umrah

WOTTT arranges Umrah only and does not arrange Hajj. Visa issuance is subject to Saudi authorities. Hotel names, distances and inclusions are confirmed in writing on your quotation.

Flights, holidays and visit visas

Fares, routes, baggage allowances and airline policies can change at any time before a ticket is issued. Holiday itineraries, hotels and inclusions depend on availability and are confirmed in writing before you pay. Visit and tourist visas are decided by the embassy or authority concerned; WOTTT prepares and lodges the file but cannot guarantee the result. Embassy and visa-centre fees are set and kept by those bodies under their own rules; the refund terms for WOTTT’s own service fee are stated on your quotation.

Travel insurance

Travel insurance is provided by the insurer named on your policy. Cover, exclusions, claim procedures and acceptance at your destination are set by that insurer’s policy wording, which you should read before you buy. Claims are made to the insurer; WOTTT can help you reach them.

Your documents

You are responsible for the documents and information you give us being genuine, complete and accurate, and for your passport being valid for travel. We cannot submit an application we know to contain false information.

Training Academy

Applying for the Academy does not reserve a seat. A seat is confirmed after a conversation with the coordinator and payment of the deposit; fees, instalments and refund terms are set out in the enrolment confirmation. Before enrolling, ask for the written fee, class schedule and cancellation or batch-transfer terms; any refund or transfer request is considered under the terms agreed for your batch. If WOTTT changes or cancels a batch, the available arrangements are communicated to enrolled students.

Responsibility

We take care to give accurate guidance and to arrange what you book as agreed. We are not responsible for decisions made by universities, embassies or authorities, or for changes made by airlines, hotels or other providers, except where the law says otherwise. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under the law of Pakistan.

Content and photographs

The text and design of this website belong to WOTTT. Photographs are used under their photographers’ licences and may not be reused from this website.

Law and disputes

These terms are subject to the applicable laws of Pakistan. Disputes will be handled by the competent courts having jurisdiction in Lahore, subject to any mandatory rights or legal requirements. If something goes wrong, please contact the branch first; most issues are resolved quickly.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as our services or the law change. The current version is always on this page, with its effective date at the top.
