Destination FAQs

Questions families ask first.

Full FAQ on each destination page.

Which countries does WOTTT actively process?

Every country carries a status: Confirmed, Consultation or Planned. Confirmed means WOTTT provides verified services there.

Can I study in Italy or Germany without IELTS?

Some programmes accept alternative evidence; rules vary by university and year. We confirm for your shortlist.

How long does a study visa take?

It depends on country, intake and appointment availability; we plan backwards from your intake date.

Do you arrange Hajj?

No — WOTTT arranges Umrah packages only.
