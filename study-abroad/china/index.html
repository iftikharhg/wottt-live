01 · Introduction

Why students from Pakistan choose China

China hosts one of the largest Pakistani student communities abroad, offers English-taught MBBS, engineering and business programmes, and funds many students through Chinese Government (CSC) scholarships and university awards. Many programmes are taught in Chinese after a preparatory language year.

WOTTT offers consultation for China case by case: profile and budget assessment, programme shortlisting, and guidance on the admission and study-visa sequence.

  1. 01Academic

    Top-ranked universities and English-taught MBBS

    Tsinghua, Peking, Fudan and Zhejiang rank among the world's leading research universities; listed medical universities teach English-medium MBBS, subject to PMDC recognition of the specific university.

  2. 02Careers

    CPEC ties and engineering demand

    For a China study plan, look closely at the course language, practical training and how the qualification fits your career in Pakistan. Learning the local language can also make everyday life easier.

  3. 03After study

    Limited stay-back; employer sponsorship

    Plan beyond the degree. WOTTT can help you identify the questions to ask about further study, professional recognition and any permission needed to work after graduation in China.

  4. 04Cost & life

    CSC scholarships; large Pakistani student community

    Chinese Government (CSC) scholarships cover tuition, housing and a stipend for many Pakistani students. Dormitories are common, costs are moderate outside Beijing and Shanghai, and Pakistani student societies are widespread.

04 · Admission

General admission considerations

  • Share your qualifications, results and preferred subject with WOTTT. We will help you compare the entry requirements and application dates for suitable programmes in China.
  • Before paying any fees for a medical or dental degree abroad, check the exact institution, programme and your own eligibility directly with PMDC. WOTTT can help you organise the documents and questions for that check.
  • A physical examination record and a police (non-criminal record) certificate are commonly required.
  • Admitted students receive an admission notice and a JW201/JW202 form for the visa.

07 · Funding

Scholarships and funding

  • Chinese Government Scholarships (CSC) — cover tuition, accommodation, insurance and a stipend; competitive, via embassy or university routes.
  • University and provincial scholarships — partial or full awards offered by universities and local governments.

In consultation we identify the schemes you may be eligible for and explain their deadlines. Scholarships are never guaranteed.

08 · Visa

Study visa overview

Students apply for an X1 study visa (for programmes longer than 180 days) with the admission notice and JW201/JW202 form, through the Chinese visa application service in Pakistan. A residence permit is obtained within a set period after arrival.

  • Admission notice and JW201/JW202 form
  • Physical examination record
  • Passport and academic documents
  • Proof of funds or scholarship letter
Guidance onlyVisa decisions rest with the Chinese embassy or consulate. WOTTT can guide you on the file; it does not guarantee an outcome.

10 · FAQ

China — questions families ask

Is an MBBS from China recognised in Pakistan?

Before paying any fees for a medical or dental degree abroad, check the exact institution, programme and your own eligibility directly with PMDC. WOTTT can help you organise the documents and questions for that check.

Do I need to learn Chinese?

Check the teaching language and the language used in placements separately. WOTTT helps you review the exact programme requirements before applying.

Can I work while studying in China?

Your permission to work depends on your visa or residence conditions. Check those conditions before taking a job, and arrange a study budget that does not depend on finding part-time work.
