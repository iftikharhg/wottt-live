Questions

About intakes.

Which intake should I aim for?

The main intake (usually September or October) has the most programmes, scholarships and seats. A second intake is useful if your documents or test result arrive late, but fewer programmes open then.

How early is “too early” to apply?

Never. Universities open applications eight to twelve months before the intake. Attestation and English tests can be started before the portal opens.

What if I miss the deadline?

Some universities accept late applications for less competitive programmes; most do not. We tell you honestly whether to wait for the next intake rather than rush an incomplete file.

Do visa appointments run out?

In peak months — June to August for a September intake — yes, at several consulates. Book as soon as you have the admission letter; we chase the appointment with you.
