01 · Introduction

Why students from Pakistan choose Turkey

Turkey combines low-fee public universities with foundation (private) universities that charge fixed fees and teach more programmes in English. Cultural familiarity, direct flights from Lahore and a large international student community make it a comfortable first move abroad for many Pakistani students.

WOTTT offers consultation for Turkey case by case: profile and budget assessment, programme shortlisting, and guidance on the admission and study-visa sequence.

  1. 01Academic

    English-taught programmes at top technical universities

    METU, Boğaziçi and Istanbul Technical teach many programmes in English at low public fees, while foundation universities such as Bilkent and Koç add scholarships for strong applicants.

  2. 02Careers

    Manufacturing, construction and tech hubs

    For Turkey, check whether your chosen course is taught in English or Turkish and how practical training is arranged. WOTTT helps compare institutions and the total cost of the programme.

  3. 03After study

    Work permit needs an employer

    Plan beyond the degree. WOTTT can help you identify the questions to ask about further study, professional recognition and any permission needed to work after graduation in Turkey.

  4. 04Cost & life

    Low fees, direct flights, familiar culture

    Public tuition is low and state dormitories keep rent down; Istanbul costs more than other cities. Direct Lahore to Istanbul flights, halal food everywhere and a large international student community ease the move.

04 · Admission

General admission considerations

  • Share your qualifications, results and preferred subject with WOTTT. We will help you compare the entry requirements and application dates for suitable programmes in Turkey.
  • Bachelor's applications rely on HSSC or A-level results; master's applications need bachelor's transcripts and sometimes ALES or GRE scores.
  • Türkiye Scholarships has its own online application with a fixed annual window.
  • Documents may need attestation and Turkish translation.
05 · Language

Language requirements

English-taught programmes ask for IELTS/TOEFL or an internal proficiency exam, and many run an English preparatory year for students below the threshold. Turkish-taught programmes require Turkish proficiency, often reached through a one-year language course.

Verify per programmeThresholds above are typical ranges, not a guarantee of acceptance.
06 · Costs

Tuition and living-cost guidance

Public university fees for international students are low and set by each university; foundation universities charge fixed, higher fees, often quoted in US dollars. Living costs are moderate and higher in Istanbul than in other cities.

07 · Funding

Scholarships and funding

  • Türkiye Scholarships (Türkiye Bursları) — government scholarships covering tuition, accommodation, a monthly stipend and a Turkish language year; very competitive.
  • Foundation university scholarships — partial to full tuition waivers based on academic results.

In consultation we identify the schemes you may be eligible for and explain their deadlines. Scholarships are never guaranteed.

08 · Visa

Study visa overview

Once you have a suitable offer, WOTTT helps you organise your student-visa documents for Turkey. We review the applicable checklist, financial evidence and any appointment or residence steps with you. Decisions and processing remain with the relevant authorities.

  • Acceptance letter from the university
  • Proof of funds or a scholarship letter
  • Passport and attested academic documents
  • Health insurance for the residence permit
Guidance onlyVisa decisions rest with the Turkish embassy or consulate. WOTTT can guide you on the file; it does not guarantee an outcome.

10 · FAQ

Turkey — questions families ask

Do I need to learn Turkish?

Not for English-taught programmes, although many include an English preparatory year; Turkish-taught programmes require a language year.

Can I work while studying in Turkey?

Your permission to work depends on your visa or residence conditions. Check those conditions before taking a job, and arrange a study budget that does not depend on finding part-time work.

Are Turkish medical degrees recognised in Pakistan?

Before paying any fees for a medical or dental degree abroad, check the exact institution, programme and your own eligibility directly with PMDC. WOTTT can help you organise the documents and questions for that check.
