01 · Introduction

Why students from Pakistan consider Kyrgyzstan

Kyrgyzstan's medical universities attract Pakistani students with English-medium MBBS programmes and low fees. Quality and recognition vary widely between universities, so careful checks come before any application.

Kyrgyzstan is under review as a WOTTT destination. We can advise on whether it fits your profile and budget, but we do not yet offer confirmed services there.

  1. 01Academic

    Low-fee English-medium MBBS

    Medical universities in Bishkek and Osh teach MBBS in English and are well known among Pakistani students; quality and PMDC recognition vary widely, so the university must be checked first.

  2. 02Careers

    Clinical exposure needs Russian

    For medicine in Kyrgyzstan, ask how clinical training is delivered and what local language learning is required. Course quality and your future licensing route matter throughout the degree.

  3. 03After study

    Return to Pakistan for PMDC licensing

    Plan beyond the degree. WOTTT can help you identify the questions to ask about further study, professional recognition and any permission needed to work after graduation in Kyrgyzstan.

  4. 04Cost & life

    Among the lowest-cost options

    Tuition and living costs are low and university hostels are common. Bishkek is a one-stop flight from Lahore, one hour ahead, in a Muslim-majority country with a large Pakistani student body.

04 · Admission

General admission considerations

  • Each university sets its own admission requirements; most intakes are in September.
  • Before paying any fees for a medical or dental degree abroad, check the exact institution, programme and your own eligibility directly with PMDC. WOTTT can help you organise the documents and questions for that check.
  • Admitted students receive an invitation to support the student visa.
05 · Language

Language requirements

MBBS programmes are taught in English, but clinical work involves Russian or Kyrgyz; learning Russian is strongly advised.

Verify per programmeThresholds above are typical ranges, not a guarantee of acceptance.

07 · Funding

Scholarships and funding

  • University discounts — Ask the university about any published early-payment or acceptance discount. Read the conditions and refund terms before paying to secure an offer.
  • External funding — most students are self-funded or family-funded.

We can point you to the main schemes during consultation. Scholarships are never guaranteed.

08 · Visa

Study visa overview

Once you have a suitable offer, WOTTT helps you organise your student-visa documents for Kyrgyzstan. We review the applicable checklist, financial evidence and any appointment or residence steps with you. Decisions and processing remain with the relevant authorities.

  • University invitation letter
  • Passport and legalised academic documents
  • Medical or health documents specified in the official student-visa checklist.
Guidance onlyVisa decisions rest with the Kyrgyz authorities. WOTTT does not process visas for this country at present and does not guarantee any outcome.
