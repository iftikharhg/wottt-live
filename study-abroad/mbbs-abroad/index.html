Read this first

Four rules that decide everything.

Every year families choose a university on price and discover the problem at registration time. These four checks come before any shortlist.

  1. RULE 1

    Recognition of the university

    The Pakistan Medical and Dental Council publishes which foreign medical colleges it recognises. A degree from a college not on the list may not let you practise in Pakistan. Check each university and programme directly against PMDC’s current official information before applying; we help you organise the documents and questions for that check.

  2. RULE 2

    MDCAT before you leave

    Rules on whether a student needs a passing MDCAT score before studying medicine abroad have changed more than once. Before paying any fees, check the current position and your own eligibility directly with PMDC; your consultant helps you prepare the questions.

  3. RULE 3

    Licensing exam on return

    Foreign graduates sit PMDC’s registration examination before they can register in Pakistan; confirm the current exam and its syllabus with PMDC. Plan for it from year one — it tests the same syllabus Pakistani graduates follow.

  4. RULE 4

    Internship and house job

    Where you complete your house job — abroad or in Pakistan — affects registration. Some countries include an internship year in the degree; some do not.

Beyond MBBS

Dentistry, pharmacy and nursing.

The same universities teach the allied degrees families ask about next. Recognition runs through a different council for each profession.

Dentistry (BDS)

Recognition works like MBBS: the college must be on the PMDC list and graduates sit the registration exam on return. Five years in most countries.

Pharmacy (PharmD / BPharm)

Registration in Pakistan is with the Pharmacy Council of Pakistan, which has its own equivalence rules for foreign degrees — a PharmD from abroad is not automatically a PharmD here.

Nursing (BSN)

The Pakistan Nursing Council registers foreign nursing graduates on equivalence; check the programme’s clinical hours and language of practice before you apply.

Questions

About MBBS abroad.

Which country is cheapest for MBBS?

Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Tajikistan are usually the lowest-fee options, followed by Kazakhstan and Azerbaijan. Cheapest is not the same as safest — recognition and teaching quality vary a lot between universities in the same country.

Is an MBBS from abroad valid in Pakistan?

Only if the university is recognised by PMDC and you pass the registration exam on return. We never recommend a university whose recognition status we cannot confirm.

Do I need MDCAT to study MBBS abroad?

The rule has changed several times. Before paying any fees for a medical or dental degree abroad, check the current requirement and your own eligibility directly with PMDC; we help you organise the documents and questions for that check.

Can I study medicine in Europe with FSc?

Yes in some countries — Latvia, Lithuania and Cyprus admit on FSc science marks plus an interview or test. Italy needs the IMAT. Fees and living costs are higher than Central Asia.

What about dentistry, pharmacy or nursing?

Most of the universities on this page also teach BDS, pharmacy and nursing — see the table above. Each profession registers through a different council in Pakistan (PMDC, the Pharmacy Council, the Nursing Council), so the recognition check is done per programme, not per university.
