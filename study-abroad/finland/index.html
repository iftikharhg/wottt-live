01 · Introduction

Why students from Pakistan choose Finland

WOTTT helps students compare Finnish university and university of applied sciences options according to their qualifications and study goals. We put the course requirements, fees and housing plan in one place.

WOTTT offers consultation for Finland case by case: profile and budget assessment, programme shortlisting, and guidance on the admission and study-visa sequence.

  1. 01Academic

    Teaching quality and applied-science options

    Finland is known for teaching quality; research universities such as Aalto and Helsinki sit alongside universities of applied sciences (UAS) that offer practical English-taught bachelor's.

  2. 02Careers

    ICT, gaming and clean-tech sector

    Compare the balance of academic study, projects and practical training when shortlisting Finland. WOTTT can help you review the programme and what it asks of international applicants.

  3. 03After study

    Post-study residence permit to find work

    Plan beyond the degree. WOTTT can help you identify the questions to ask about further study, professional recognition and any permission needed to work after graduation in Finland.

  4. 04Cost & life

    Scholarships common on non-EU tuition

    Most institutions offer scholarships covering part or all of non-EU tuition, often renewed on progress. Living costs are high but student housing foundations keep rent down; winters are long and dark.

04 · Admission

General admission considerations

  • Share your qualifications, results and preferred subject with WOTTT. We will help you compare the entry requirements and application dates for suitable programmes in Finland.
  • UAS bachelor's may require an entrance exam; universities assess transcripts and a motivation letter.
  • A recognised bachelor's in a related field is needed for master's entry; some programmes ask for GRE/GMAT.
  • Admitted students confirm their place and pay first-year tuition (less any scholarship) before the residence permit.

07 · Funding

Scholarships and funding

  • University scholarships — partial or full tuition waivers offered by most institutions, often renewed on academic progress.
  • Finland Scholarship — WOTTT can help you look for published scholarships and fee reductions for Finland. Each award has its own eligibility rules, coverage and deadline; funding is confirmed by the awarding body.
  • Early-acceptance discounts — Ask the university about any published early-payment or acceptance discount. Read the conditions and refund terms before paying to secure an offer.

In consultation we identify the schemes you may be eligible for and explain their deadlines. Scholarships are never guaranteed.

08 · Visa

Study visa overview

Students apply online for a first residence permit for studies after admission, then visit a Finnish mission or visa centre to prove their identity. Proof of funds and health insurance are required.

  • Letter of admission and proof of tuition payment
  • Financial evidence in the format and amount specified in the official student-visa checklist.
  • Health insurance meeting permit conditions
  • Passport and academic documents
Guidance onlyVisa decisions rest with the Finnish Immigration Service. WOTTT can guide you on the file; it does not guarantee an outcome.

10 · FAQ

Finland — questions families ask

Can I work while studying in Finland?

Your permission to work depends on your visa or residence conditions. Check those conditions before taking a job, and arrange a study budget that does not depend on finding part-time work.

University or university of applied sciences?

Universities are research-oriented; universities of applied sciences are practical and career-focused. We recommend based on your goals and background.

Can I stay after graduating?

Discuss the options after graduation with WOTTT as part of your course planning. Eligibility depends on the qualification, your circumstances and the immigration rules in force when you apply.
