Getting started

When should I start planning?

Nine to twelve months before your intake. Attestation, English tests and scholarship deadlines all take longer than families expect.

Which country is best for me?

It depends on your grades, degree length, budget, field and plans after study. In the first consultation we compare two or three realistic options side by side.

Do you charge for the first consultation?

No. Your first consultation with WOTTT is free. We discuss your profile, study goal and the next steps before you decide whether to proceed.

Eligibility and tests

Can I apply for a master’s with a 14-year bachelor’s?

Some countries and universities accept it for certain programmes or with a bridging year; many expect 16 years. We check your transcript against each university’s rules.

Do I need IELTS?

Usually an English test is required, but some universities accept PTE, TOEFL, Duolingo or a medium-of-instruction letter instead. See the English tests guide.

Is a gap in my studies a problem?

Not necessarily. A gap explained with work experience or other activity is common; we help you present it honestly in your statement.

Costs and funding

How much money do I need to show for the visa?

Each country sets its own amount and format — some ask for bank statements, others a blocked account. Your consultant gives you the current figure for your destination.

Can I work while I study?

Most destinations allow limited part-time work during term. It helps with living costs but should never be counted on to pay tuition.

Are scholarships available for students from Pakistan?

Yes — government schemes, university awards and regional grants. None is guaranteed; see the scholarships page for each destination.

Visas

Do you guarantee the visa?

No. Visa decisions belong to embassies and consulates; no honest consultant can promise one. We prepare a complete, consistent file and a mock interview.

What if my visa is refused?

We go through the refusal reasons with you and advise whether a fresh application makes sense and what would need to change.

Can my family come with me?

Rules on dependants differ by country and level of study and change often. We explain the current position for your destination in consultation.

ConfirmedGuide →
Is studying in Germany really free?

Most public universities charge no tuition for most programmes, but there is a semester contribution, Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU students tuition, and you must prove living costs for the visa.

Can I work while studying in Germany?

Your permission to work depends on your visa or residence conditions. Check those conditions before taking a job, and arrange a study budget that does not depend on finding part-time work.

Do I need to speak German?

Not for English-taught programmes, but basic German makes part-time work, housing and daily life much easier — and most bachelor's degrees are taught in German only.
