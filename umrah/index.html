Umrah FAQ

Questions families ask first.

How far in advance should we book?

Contact WOTTT as early as you can, especially for Ramadan and school holidays. More time gives us a better chance to compare flights, hotel locations and room options that suit your family.

Can elders travel with wheelchair assistance?

Yes — airport assistance, Haram-adjacent hotels and wheelchair arrangements for Tawaf are built into the Haram View tier and available as an add-on elsewhere.

Do you arrange Hajj?

No. WOTTT arranges Umrah only, and does not process Hajj visas or packages.

What is the payment and cancellation policy?

Cancellation charges depend on the flights, hotels, visa work and other services already booked. WOTTT will state the deposit, balance due date and applicable refund terms in your quotation before payment. Ask us to explain any non-refundable item before confirming.
