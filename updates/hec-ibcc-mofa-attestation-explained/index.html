HEC, IBCC and MOFA attestation explained

Which document goes to which office, in what order, how long it usually takes, and the mistakes that send families back to the start.

Reviewed by the study team: November 2026

Ask a consultant

Guides are general. Your file is specific.

Ask about your documents

Document preparation is easier when you know what each institution asks for. WOTTT helps you make a checklist of certificates, transcripts, attestations and translations, then track what is ready and what is still needed.

Which document goes where

  • IBCC (Inter Boards Coordination Commission) attests school-level documents: Matric and Intermediate certificates and result cards, and the equivalence certificates issued for O and A Levels. Equivalence must be issued before attestation.
  • HEC (Higher Education Commission) attests degrees and transcripts from recognised Pakistani universities: bachelor’s, master’s and above. It does not attest school certificates, and it will not attest a degree from an institution that is not on its recognised list.
  • Where MOFA attestation is requested, check the current official submission route and which prior attestations are needed. Keep your original documents and receipts organised.

The order

  1. Obtain equivalence from IBCC if you studied O or A Levels, or any non-Pakistani school curriculum.
  2. Check the university and visa requirements before arranging attestation. WOTTT can help you separate school-level documents from degree documents and plan the submission order.
  3. Obtain the document checklist from the university and the relevant visa authority. Arrange only the attestations and translations requested for your application.
  4. If MOFA attestation is required, follow its current official instructions and keep the submission receipt. Allow time for any prior attestation or correction.
  5. Arrange certified translations last, so the translation covers the final attested version.

Common mistakes

  • Names that do not match across CNIC, passport and certificates. Even a missing middle name or a different spelling of Muhammad can cause a rejection at the embassy stage. Correct the certificate or passport first.
  • Attesting the degree certificate but not the transcript, or the other way round. Universities almost always want both, and some want a provisional certificate as well.
  • Submitting photocopies where originals are required, or laminated originals that cannot be stamped.
  • Going to MOFA before HEC or IBCC. MOFA stamps only what the earlier office has already attested.
  • Leaving attestation until after the admission letter arrives. By then the visa appointment is weeks away and there is no slack.
NoteFees, online portals and the list of countries that require MOFA or an apostille change without much notice. Check the IBCC, HEC and MOFA websites, or ask a WOTTT consultant for the current process, before you travel to an office.
