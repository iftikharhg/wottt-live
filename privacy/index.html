How we use your details.

What WOTTT collects when you contact us through this website, why, who sees it and how to ask us to change or delete it.

Effective 16 November 2026 · applies to wottt.com.pk and to enquiries made through it

Who we are

Wolf of the Travel Trade (“WOTTT”, “we”), trading as WOTTT Travel & Tours, Lahore (DTS Licence No. 12267). Head office: 29-G Block, 1st Floor, DHA Phase 1, Lahore 54792. For anything in this policy, write to info@wottt.com.pk, call +92 311 1123381 or +92 311 1200554, or message us on WhatsApp.

What we collect

Only what you give us, and a few technical details your browser sends:

  • Enquiry forms and the chat box: your name, WhatsApp number, email if you give one, the service you ask about, your preferred branch, and the details you type — for example education level, intake, travel dates, number of travellers, budget band or a message.
  • Where you came from: the page you were on, the page that linked to it, and campaign tags in the web address (such as utm_source or gclid), so we know which pages and adverts bring enquiries.
  • Spam protection: a one-way fingerprint of your network address, used only to limit repeated submissions. We cannot recover the address from it.
  • What we do not collect: passport scans, payment card details or documents through this website. Those are exchanged only after you speak to a consultant.

Why we use it

To reply to you and give the guidance or quotation you asked for; to keep a record of your enquiry in our customer system so any branch can pick it up; to follow up on your enquiry; and to understand which services and pages people use. We do not sell your details.

Who we share it with

Inside WOTTT, your enquiry is visible to the staff of the branch and desk that handle it. Outside WOTTT, we share details only when needed for the service you choose, and only once you have asked us to proceed:

  • universities, colleges and application portals you apply to;
  • embassies, consulates and visa application centres, and Saudi authorities for Umrah visas;
  • airlines, hotels, transport and insurance providers for bookings you confirm;
  • the companies that run our website, customer system and messaging (hosting, database and WhatsApp), which process data on our instructions.

WhatsApp

When you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy also apply to that conversation. We keep the messages in our customer system so your consultant has the full history.

How long we keep it

We keep routine enquiry records for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete or anonymise them unless there is a continuing service need or a legal reason to retain them. Booking, payment and statutory records follow the retention requirements that apply to those records. You can ask us to delete an enquiry at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it or stop contacting you. Write to info@wottt.com.pk or message us on WhatsApp from the number you used; use the subject “Privacy request”; we aim to acknowledge within 3 working days and give a full response within 30 calendar days, or explain why more time is needed.

Cookies and analytics

The website itself sets no tracking cookies. If we add an analytics service, this section will name it and a notice will ask for your consent where required. The chat box keeps a small record in your browser so your conversation is still there when you return.

The Umrah package builder, the budget planner and the checklists also save your choices in your own browser, so they are still there when you come back. Those choices reach WOTTT only if you submit the form or send them on WhatsApp.

The live map on the contact page is provided by Google Maps and loads only when you choose a branch on it. From that moment Google’s own terms and privacy policy apply to the map.

Stories, photos and videos

Client stories, photographs and videos on this website are published with the permission of the people in them. If you appear in one and would like it changed or removed, write to info@wottt.com.pk or message us on WhatsApp and we will take it down.

Keeping it safe

Your details travel over an encrypted connection and are stored in our customer system, where only staff who need them can see them. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

Students under 18

For students under 18, a parent or guardian should contact us or be included in the conversation.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your details, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Questions

Ask us directly.

Any branch can help, or write to info@wottt.com.pk.
