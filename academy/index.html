Curriculum

Fifteen classes, in the order the work happens.in order.

Each class is a working session on real files — the same forms, trackers and supplier portals used across WOTTT's branches. Titles below are the planned syllabus; the academy confirms the final schedule per batch.

  • Module AFoundations · classes 1–4
  • Module BService lines · classes 5–10
  • Module CRunning the business · classes 11–15

Module A · Foundations
  1. 01The travel trade in PakistanModule AHow agencies, IATA, consolidators, consultants and sub-agents fit together; where the margin is.
  2. 02Setting up: licence, registration, suppliersModule ACompany registration, travel-agency licensing, bank and supplier onboarding, what to outsource.
  3. 03The consultationModule AProfiling a client, qualifying budget and intent, saying no, and when to hand over to a specialist.
  4. 04Documents, attestation and complianceModule AAttestation chains, translations, financial evidence, and the rule: never promise an outcome.
Module B · Service lines
  1. 05Study abroad I — pathways and shortlistingModule BItaly, Germany, UK, China and emerging markets: what each accepts, intakes, how to build a shortlist.
  2. 06Study abroad II — applications and scholarshipsModule BPortals, pre-enrolment, motivation letters, scholarship schemes and their deadlines.
  3. 07Study visas, country by countryModule BFile structure, financial proof, appointment systems, interview preparation.
  4. 08Visit visas — Schengen, UK, Gulf, AsiaModule BCover letters, itineraries, sponsorship, refusals and reapplications.
  5. 09Umrah operationsModule BVisa processing, hotel contracting, group handling, elders and families, what not to sell.
  6. 10Ticketing, insurance and holiday packagingModule BReading fares and rules, GDS basics, insurance that embassies accept, building a package quote.
Module C · Running the business
  1. 11Lead management and CRMModule CWhatsApp-first intake, lead sheets, follow-up cadence, and turning enquiries into files.
  2. 12Digital marketing for agenciesModule CSocial content that brings the right enquiries, ads basics, the website as a sales tool.
  3. 13Pricing, ledgers and cash flowModule CService fees vs commissions, client and vendor ledgers, deposits, refunds and disputes.
  4. 14Hiring and running a branchModule CConsultant profiles, training, attendance, targets, and the manager's weekly rhythm.
  5. 15Your 90-day launch planModule CEach trainee leaves with a written plan, supplier list and first-quarter targets; review call at day 30.

Academy FAQ

Before you apply.

Do I need travel experience to join?

No. Module A starts from the structure of the trade. Existing agents can skip to Module B by arrangement.

Can I attend online from another city?

Ask the WOTTT Academy coordinator about the available classroom or online format and access to any course recordings for your batch.

Will WOTTT hire graduates?

Strong graduates are considered for consultant openings at WOTTT branches when vacancies arise; it is not guaranteed.

What is the fee and can it be paid in instalments?

Contact WOTTT for the current course fee and payment schedule. We will explain what is included before you enrol.
